# Sound asset provenance

## Starter kit v1 (`sonora.starter-kit.v1`)

All eight starter samples are generated by Sonora's own DSP code in
`src/DrumSampler.cpp`. No recordings, downloaded sample packs, or third-party
sound assets are used. The generators are part of this project's source and
follow its licensing choice; no additional sample-pack license is required.

### Factory variants (Sonora 0.12+)

The Deep, Crisp, Tight, Boom, and Warm kits re-voice the same recipes with pitch/decay/seed
parameters (`KitVariant` in `DrumSampler.h`). They are generated, not recorded,
and share the starter kit's licensing. The default variant reproduces the
original v1 buffers bit-exactly.

The constructor generates deterministic mono buffers at 48 kHz, using analytic
oscillators, envelopes, and a locally seeded noise generator. Buffers are created
before the audio callback starts, normalized to restrained levels, and kept
immutable. The sampler interpolates them at the active device rate.

| Pad | Construction | MIDI channel 10 note | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kick | Pitch-decaying sine and short noise transient | 36 | Q |
| Snare | Damped sine body and filtered noise | 38 | W |
| Closed hat | Short high-frequency noise/oscillator decay | 42 | E |
| Open hat | Longer high-frequency noise/oscillator decay | 46 | R |
| Clap | Clustered filtered-noise bursts and tail | 39 | A |
| Low tom | Pitch-decaying sine plus inharmonic partial | 45 | S |
| Rim | Two damped oscillators and noise transient | 37 | D |
| Shaker | Shaped high-frequency noise | 82 | F |

Closed/open hats share a choke group with a short fade. The keyboard mapping is
active in the drum editor. Project files store the instrument identifier and
step velocities rather than duplicate sample data. Preserve the v1 generator
when adding future kits, or introduce a new kit identifier and an explicit migration.

## User-imported samples (Sonora 0.11+)

Samples loaded through the Kit panel are the user's own files: Sonora copies
them into the project's media folder but claims no license over them. Check
the sample pack's license before distributing a project containing them.
Missing files fall back to the starter pads above rather than failing the load.
